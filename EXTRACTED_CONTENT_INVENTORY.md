## 10. Briefing Estratégico para Generador de Prompts (Prompt Generator Inputs)

### 1. The Core Offer & Conversion Goal
- **What exactly are you selling or offering?**
  APG Química offers the B2B commercialization, technical distribution, and custom formulation support of specialized chemical products, natural raw materials, and high-performance industrial additives across 12 target manufacturing industries: Metalworking/Metallurgy, Additives & Flame Retardants, Crop Protection & Agrochemistry, Coatings & Paints, Ceramics & Tile Glazes, Industrial Water Treatment, Sustainable Construction, Synthetic/Montan Waxes, High-Performance Pigments, Leather & Footwear (tanning/adhesives), Home Care & Institutional Cleaning (I&I), and Personal Care/Cosmetics.
  - **Flagship Specialty Line:** Antifrogen® (by Clariant), an industrial heat-transfer fluid and antifreeze with corrosion inhibitors free of carcinogenic, mutagenic, or reprotoxic (CMR) substances, alongside food-grade Antifrogen L.
  - **In-House Technical Services:** Dedicated technical support laboratory delivering batch application testing, stability testing, and physicochemical quality measurements.
  - **Brand Backing:** Official representative of Clariant (global leader in specialty and sustainable chemicals).
- **What is the single primary action you want a user to take?**
  **Requesting a Specialized Technical Quote / Formulation Consultation ("Solicitar Cotización / Asesoría Técnica Especializada")** via an interactive lead form or direct 1-click WhatsApp/phone connection with a technical sales engineer. *(No direct e-commerce purchase).*

### 2. Target Audience & Psychological Drivers
- **Who is your ideal customer?**
  B2B industrial procurement heads, technical plant managers, R&D chemists, and chemical formulation engineers in manufacturing enterprises across Mexico and LATAM (specifically in automotive/machining, agricultural crop protection, coatings, plastics, construction materials, cosmetics, and industrial cleaning).
- **What emotional state are they in when they arrive at your site?**
  **Analytical, risk-averse, and problem-focused / solution-seeking.** They often arrive with urgent technical bottlenecks (e.g., foaming in production, thermal degradation, unstable emulsions, corrosion in heating/cooling loops, or strict environmental bans on CMR chemicals), supply disruptions, or a mandate to transition to sustainable, eco-friendly raw materials without compromising yield or cycle time.
- **What is their biggest hesitation or objection before buying?**
  - **Risk of formulation incompatibility or production stoppage:** *"Will this batch perform consistently in our machinery and specific chemical matrix?"*
  - **Regulatory & safety non-compliance:** *"Does this chemical meet environmental, non-CMR, and quality certifications?"*
  - **Vendor reliability & technical backing:** *"Are we dealing with an unverified distributor, or do they offer real laboratory support, lot traceability, and certified backing from top-tier chemical manufacturers?"*

### 3. Authority & Trust Signals
- **What makes you the undeniable choice?**
  1. **20+ Years of Proven Market Track Record:** Two decades of continuous supply to Mexico's leading industrial sectors.
  2. **Official Representation of Clariant:** Partnership with a global giant in sustainable specialty chemicals, providing certified provenance and technical documentation.
  3. **Proprietary Technical Support Laboratory:** In-house facility equipped for rigorous real-world application testing, accelerated stability tests, and physicochemical verification prior to delivery.
  4. **Strict Quality & Hygiene Protocol:** Documented quality controls across manufacturing, handling, and storage environments.
  5. **Certified CMR-Free & Sustainable Options:** High-safety profiles (e.g., Antifrogen N with zero carcinogens, mutagens, or reproductive toxins; 100% natural sugar-based surfactants).
  6. **Strategic Logistics Hub:** Centrally located in Querétaro's industrial manufacturing heartland.
